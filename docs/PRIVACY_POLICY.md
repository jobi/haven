# Privacy Policy for Haven

**Last Updated: August 2026**

Haven is built from the ground up with a strict privacy-first philosophy.

## 1. Zero Data Collection
Haven does not collect, store, track, sell, or transmit any personal data, analytics, diagnostics, crash reports, or telemetry to third parties or remote servers owned by the developers.

## 2. Direct Communication
Haven communicates directly with the Home Assistant server instance(s) that you configure. No traffic passes through any intermediate proxy or third-party servers operated by Haven.

## 3. Secure Credential Storage
All authentication tokens, server addresses, and preferences are stored locally on your device in the Apple iOS Keychain and local app storage. This data never leaves your device except when authenticating directly with your specified Home Assistant instance.

## 4. Camera and Media Streams
WebRTC and HLS video streaming feeds are established directly between your device and your Home Assistant server or cloud camera provider (such as Google Nest SDM). Haven developers have no access to your video or audio feeds.

## 5. Contact
If you have questions about this privacy policy, you can open an issue on the GitHub repository: https://github.com/jobi/haven/issues
